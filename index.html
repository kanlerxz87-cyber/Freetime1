WHAT “FREE” MEANS

No listed game is not proof of free time

A genuinely free minute requires all qualifying events and radio broadcasts to be absent during that minute. Scheduled start times alone do not establish game end times.

SCHEDULE STATUS

Confirmed facts and gaps are separated

Official schedules and future opponent announcements are linked directly. Unreleased matchups, missing kickoffs, and station carriage remain blank and flagged.
