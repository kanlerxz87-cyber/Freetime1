Bottom line for vacation planning: Under the strict rule (no MLB including Spring Training, no NFL including preseason, no Stanford/Cal, no Westwood One), there is no 1-week, 2-week, or 3-week completely game-free window in 2027, 2028, or 2029. The longest strict all-off run is 4–5 days in February (after the Super Bowl, before Spring Training). If Spring Training/preseason do not count, the practical vacation window is mid-February → late March (5–6 weeks). See Vacation Windows and Executive Summary.

📻 What counts as “busy”?

A minute is busy when any of these is on air: MLB (Giants & A’s HIGH, all 30 clubs shown) · NFL / 49ers HIGH · Stanford / Cal NCAAF HIGH · Westwood One national NFL radio. Otherwise it is free.
