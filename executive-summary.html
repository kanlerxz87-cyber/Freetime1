When — and what days — have no games on Bay Area AM/FM radio · 2026–2029 · Pacific time

Headline answer: If “free” means no MLB (including Spring Training), no NFL (including preseason), no Stanford/Cal, no Westwood One, then no 7-day, 14-day, or 21-day vacation window exists in 2027, 2028, or 2029. The longest strict all-off run is 4–5 days in February. Plan vacations around that fact — or explicitly exclude Spring Training/preseason (see §4).

1. What “free” means (one rule, applied everywhere)

A minute is FREE when none of these are on air; otherwise it is BUSY:

  • MLB — Giants & Athletics HIGH PRIORITY; all 30 clubs shown (a game is on almost every day in season).
  • NFL — 49ers HIGH PRIORITY; all 32 teams shown (preseason + regular + postseason).
  • NCAAF — Stanford & Cal HIGH PRIORITY (regular season + bowls/CFP window).
  • Westwood One Sports national NFL radio (TNF/SNF/MNF, holiday tripleheaders, all postseason).

Radio stations in scope (Outer Sunset 94122, standard AM/FM): KNBR 680 AM / 104.5 FM (Giants, 49ers, Stanford; Westwood One charter affiliate), KSAN 107.7 FM (49ers), KTCT 1050 AM (KNBR overflow), KGO 810 AM (49ers network), KSFN 1510 AM (Giants simulcast). See Radio Guide. Athletics Bay Area AM/FM carriage in 2026–2027 is unverified (Sacramento-based) — flagged, not assumed.

Planning durations (radio on-air window = 30-min pregame + game): MLB 150 min, NFL 192 min (halftime 12 min), NCAAF 204 min (halftime 20 min). Sources and ranges in Methodology.

There is no 7-day strict window in 2027–2029.

4. Practical vacation guidance (if preseason doesn’t count)

Many listeners do not treat Spring Training / NFL preseason as “must-listen” radio. Under that regular-season-only rule (MLB regular + postseason, NFL regular + postseason, NCAAF), the picture flips:

Recommendation: For a 1–3 week vacation with zero regular-season radio conflicts, book late February → mid/late March (after the Super Bowl, before Opening Day). For zero conflicts including Spring Training, the only strict option is the 4–5 day February gap — a long weekend, not a multi-week trip. Confirm Spring Training start dates on mlb.com/schedule once 2028/2029 schedules publish.
