Why there is no summer or fall window

  • Mar–Oct: MLB regular season + postseason (games almost daily across 30 clubs) → no all-off week.
  • Aug–Jan: NFL (Sun/Thu/Mon) + NCAAF Saturdays overlap MLB → no all-off week.
  • July All-Star break: Only 1–2 consecutive free days (All-Star Game itself is busy).
  • Nov–Dec: NFL + NCAAF + MLB postseason tail → no all-off week.
  • Only February sits between the Super Bowl and Spring Training/Opening Day.
