How to read this

  • FREE DAY = no MLB, no NFL, no Stanford/Cal, no Westwood One game that day. The whole day is free (1,440 min).
  • BUSY DAY = at least one game. The timeline shows the on-air window(s) and the exact free gaps before/between/after them. Example: games 1:05–3:35 PM and 6:45–9:15 PM PT → free 12:00 AM–1:05 PM, 3:35–6:45 PM, 9:15 PM–12:00 AM.
  • Verified vs. typical: 2026 Stanford/Cal/Westwood/49ers rows use published kickoffs. All other blocks are typical windows (ESTIMATED) — the league has not published that day’s exact time. Never treat a typical window as a confirmed schedule.
  • Durations: MLB 150 min · NFL 192 min · NCAAF 204 min · +30-min radio pregame. See Methodology.
