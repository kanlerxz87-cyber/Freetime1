Executive summary

  1. No week-long window is free in 2027, 2028, or 2029. Under your rules, the NFL covers roughly September through January, and MLB covers spring training through October. A 7-day, 14-day, or 21-day window does not exist in the data we can verify or estimate.
  2. The only free stretches are short. The longest verified free run is Feb 15-18, 2027 (4 days): after Super Bowl LXI (Feb 14, 2027) and before the first 2027 spring-training game (Feb 19, 2027, MLB Stats API). The same shape repeats as estimates in Feb 14-17, 2028 and Feb 12-15, 2029, because those schedules are not released yet.
  3. One 1-day gap in 2027: Wednesday, July 14, 2027. The MLB Stats API lists no games that day. The 2027 All-Star Game (July 13, Wrigley Field) is in the feed. July 12 is an estimated busy day, because the Home Run Derby is not in the MLB feed.
  4. MLB 2027 is verified. The regular-season schedule (Opening Night Mar 24, 2027) was released by MLB on July 16, 2026. Game start times marked TBD are estimated (13:00 PT day, 19:00 PT night) and flagged.
  5. NFL is only partly loaded. The NFL-related rows are the Westwood One national broadcasts (35 games) and the 49ers' official schedule. The rest of the league is treated as an estimated season window, so no free day is claimed on an NFL-season day.
  6. Radio: 49ers games are on KSAN 107.7 FM / KNBR 104.5 FM / 680 AM; Stanford is on KNBR/KTCT 1050 AM; Giants games are on KNBR 680 AM / 104.5 FM. Cal's station (810 AM) and the Athletics' Bay Area station are secondary-source only.
  7. Irregularities: Westwood One and the NFL/49ers schedules disagree on several game times (for example, 49ers at Chargers, Dec 17: 4:30 PM PT vs 5:15 PM PT). See Flagged for Review.
