Official page: westwoodonesports.com/nfl-schedule — always check the live page for the current season. Westwood One is the NFL’s official network audio partner (~500 stations; KNBR is a charter affiliate). Pattern: every Thursday Night, Sunday Night, Monday Night, holiday tripleheaders, Black Friday, Saturday doubleheaders (late season), and every postseason game through the Super Bowl. Westwood One also carries NCAA Football/Basketball, golf, soccer, etc. — only the NFL radio slate is in the vacation rule (other sports are out of scope; see Flagged).
