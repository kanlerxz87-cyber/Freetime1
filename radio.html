CATEGORY · TERRESTRIAL RADIO

Will the radio carry it?

The schedule and radio carriage are different facts. This page records evidence for stations that can be tuned in the San Francisco market and clearly distinguishes official station-finder listings from game-by-game clearances.

!
Station-finder listing does not guarantee Outer Sunset reception or a specific game.

AM/FM propagation, antenna, signal strength, game rights, preemption, and affiliate scheduling can vary. This project does not claim to have tested reception in 94122.
