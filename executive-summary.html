1. What would count as “free” (coverage is not complete)

A minute could be certified FREE only after confirming that no required event or broadcast is on air and that all relevant schedules are complete. That completeness condition is not currently met; current gaps are UNKNOWN, not free:

  • MLB — Giants & Athletics HIGH PRIORITY; all 30 clubs shown (a game is on almost every day in season).
  • NFL — 49ers HIGH PRIORITY; all 32 teams shown (preseason + regular + postseason).
  • NCAAF — Stanford & Cal HIGH PRIORITY (regular season + bowls/CFP window).
  • Westwood One Sports national NFL radio (TNF/SNF/MNF, holiday tripleheaders, all postseason).

Radio stations in scope (Outer Sunset 94122, standard AM/FM): KNBR 680 AM / 104.5 FM (Giants, 49ers, Stanford; Westwood One charter affiliate), KSAN 107.7 FM (49ers), KTCT 1050 AM (KNBR overflow), KGO 810 AM (49ers network), KSFN 1510 AM (Giants simulcast). See Radio Guide. Athletics Bay Area AM/FM carriage in 2026–2027 is unverified (Sacramento-based) — flagged, not assumed.

Planning durations (radio on-air window = 30-min pregame + game): MLB 150 min, NFL 192 min (halftime 12 min), NCAAF 204 min (halftime 20 min). Sources and ranges in Methodology.

No full day or multi-week window is certified free by this dataset. It does not ingest every future MLB fixture, complete future NFL/Stanford/Cal schedules, and every applicable radio carriage detail. Model-generated empty days are unknown, not proof of no sports. 6. Risks & what to re-check

  • 2028 MLB All-Star day unannounced on the official MLB future-locations source; do not rely on non-MLB reported dates.
  • Cal 2027 is partial. Official announcements list three nonconference games; one date has a BYU/Old Dominion conflict requiring review.
  • Westwood One Sep–Oct 2026 rows not in snapshot — use the live official page.
