2. How Minute-by-Minute PST Free Time Is Calculated

  • Time Standard (PST UTC-8): Per your requirement ("use PST as the time for this calendar schedule scoreboard"), every daily scoreboard timeline runs from 12:00 AM PST (minute 0) to 12:00 AM PST (minute 1,440) in fixed Pacific Standard Time (UTC-8), with local civil PT (PDT UTC-7 in summer) also noted on individual MLB rows for convenience.
  • Interval Union & Exact Free Complements: For any selected date and listener tier, overlapping broadcasts are merged into non-overlapping busy intervals $[s_i, e_i]$, and the exact complement intervals inside $[0, 1440]$ are reported down to the minute (e.g., if games run 12:05 PM – 2:43 PM PST and 5:45 PM – 8:23 PM PST, your free time is 12:00 AM – 12:05 PM, 2:43 PM – 5:45 PM, and 8:23 PM – 12:00 AM PST).
  • Halftime Visibility: Football (12m NFL, 20m NCAAF, ~28m Super Bowl), basketball (15m), and soccer (15m) halftimes are displayed on every scoreboard card and row, while keeping the full broadcast busy (since radio halftime shows remain live on air).
  • Optional Radio Pregame/Postgame Toggle: Toggle "Include +30m Radio Pregame & +15m Postgame" on the PST Scoreboard to expand every busy block for KNBR / 95.7 The Game / Westwood One pregame and postgame coverage.
