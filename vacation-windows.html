Why no span can be certified from this snapshot

  • MLB 2027 regular-season dates are published, but the local snapshot does not contain a complete event-by-event MLB/radio calendar.
  • The official NFL 2027–2029 schedule pages returned no schedule data at the last check; full Stanford/Cal dated schedules and radio assignments are also incomplete.
  • All-Star, postseason, Spring Training, bowls/CFP and standalone-radio coverage must be verified individually before evaluating a vacation interval.
  • Therefore this model cannot establish that any specific day is free—or conclusively rule out a 7-, 14- or 21-day interval.
