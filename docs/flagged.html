C. Estimates (not released schedules)

  • C2. MLB 2027 postseason: not yet released. Estimated window Sep 27 to Oct 31, 2027.
  • C3. NFL 2027-2029 preseason, regular season, and postseason dates are estimates (from the calendar pattern, not a released schedule). Super Bowl LXII (2028) and LXIII (2029) are estimated as the second Sunday of February.
  • C4. Stanford/Cal 2027-2029 season windows are estimates.
  • C5. The free-run results for 2028 and 2029 depend on C1-C3 and will change when the official schedules are released.
  • C6. Game times marked TBD on MLB's feed: estimated at 1:00 PM PT (day) or 7:00 PM PT (night). These are labeled "est." on every card.

D. Site or data limits

  • D1. The MLB live feed is read in the visitor's browser (statsapi.mlb.com). If the browser cannot reach it, MLB is treated as an estimated busy window everywhere, and no MLB day is shown as free.
  • D2. The MLB feed was checked by reading its responses in review; the live browser fetch itself was not exercised in this build environment.
  • D3. Free-time blocks use average game lengths, not actual end times. Pre-game and post-game time is not added.
  • D4. Reception of AM/FM stations at the Outer Sunset address (94122) was not tested.
