Free-Time Calculation Methodology

  • Timezone Normalization: All UTC timestamps from statsapi.mlb.com and Eastern Time kickoffs from nfl.com / westwoodonesports.com are converted via Python's zoneinfo.ZoneInfo("America/Los_Angeles"), automatically handling PST (UTC−8) and PDT (UTC−7, DST transitions Mar 14, 2027 & Nov 7, 2027).
  • TBD Start-Time Policy: Official games whose exact start times have not yet been published (such as 594 of 2,430 official 2027 MLB games with startTimeTBD: true) are NEVER presented as verified times. They are labeled TBD (Est. ...) using standard home-park local start windows (weekday night 6:45–7:10 PM local, Saturday 4:05 PM local, Sunday/Thursday getaway 1:10 PM local) so daily free-time estimates remain realistic while clearly flagged.
  • Interval Merging: Overlapping games on any date are merged into union busy intervals across 00:00–24:00 Pacific Time; the complement intervals are displayed as exact minute-level FREE TIME windows.
  • Mathematical Verification: Every data build is validated by scripts/verify_data.py checking exact 2,430 unique official 2027 MLB games (all 30 clubs × 162 games), exact 109 remaining 2026 NFL Weeks 12–18 games, and 100% https:// source-URL coverage across all rows.
